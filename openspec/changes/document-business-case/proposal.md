# Proposal: Document Business Case for Contractor CMS

## Problem

Contractors are skilled at their trade (construction, plumbing, consulting, etc.) but struggle with work management. Their obligations are scattered across emails, memory, spreadsheets, and note-taking tools, leading to:

- **Forgotten deadlines** — work falls through the cracks when not centralized
- **Poor client communication** — clients get no status updates unless they chase the contractor via email or phone
- **Damaged reputation** — lack of visibility creates anxiety for clients and erodes trust
- **Operational inefficiency** — time wasted searching for what they owe each client

This fragmentation costs contractors in reputation, client retention, and repeat business.

## Solution

Build a **dead-simple work tracking and client visibility system** that:

1. **For contractors:** Provides a centralized view of all obligations to each client, requiring only 5-10 minutes to update project status
2. **For clients:** Offers a simple, read-only view of their project progress without having to ask for updates

The system is intentionally minimal to solve the core problem without adding friction for contractors who dislike admin work.

## Why Now

Contractors increasingly lose business and satisfaction due to poor communication. A lightweight tool that doesn't feel like "business management overhead" can meaningfully improve reputation and client retention by making visibility effortless.

## Success Metrics

- Contractors can update status across all projects in 5-10 minutes
- Clients have current visibility into project progress without requesting updates
- Contractor reputation improves through proactive client communication
- Reduced back-and-forth emails/calls for status requests

## Scope

**MVP includes:**
- Contractor dashboard showing all projects/obligations
- Simple status update interface (5-10 min total)
- Read-only client view of project progress
- Auto-notification of status updates to clients

**Out of scope (gather client feedback first):**
- Client account/login portal (simple read-only view initially)
- Time tracking or billing
- Complex reporting or analytics
- Role-based access control
